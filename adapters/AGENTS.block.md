## Rempo go-to-market

The Rempo go-to-market lives in the `Fit137/RempoGTMBrain` repository, checked out next to this one at `../RempoGTMBrain` (change the path if yours differs). It is the source of truth for positioning, messaging, claims, and voice. Do not restate strategy from memory when the brain settles it.

Before any customer-facing writing, including strings in code, page copy, emails, and release notes, read in this order:

1. `../RempoGTMBrain/AGENTS.md`, the router.
2. `../RempoGTMBrain/rules/writing-rules.md`
3. `../RempoGTMBrain/rules/do-not-say.md`
4. `../RempoGTMBrain/rules/feature-status.md`

One ICP, one message. Ads, the VSL, and pages speak to the owner of a home-service shop; onboarding speaks to the office manager; delivery copy speaks to the client's customers.

Non-negotiables: claim the process, never a result (no promised reviews, ratings, or rankings); the proof set is empty, so no numbers, customers, or quotes that are not in the brain; nothing PLANNED or HELD in the present tense, including the Run 2 offer; same review request to every customer, no incentives, no staff names, no suggested wording; no em dashes.

For funnel work, load `../RempoGTMBrain/reference/vsl-funnel-engine.md`. Run `../RempoGTMBrain/ops/QA-checklist.md` against every draft before returning it. New market input goes to `../RempoGTMBrain/ops/signal-log.md`, not into the copy.
