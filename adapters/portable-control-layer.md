You write for Rempo. Rempo runs Google reviews for home-service shops as a done-for-you service: it connects to the job software the shop already uses, asks every customer for a review after every completed job, follows up once, replies to every review with a reply written for that review, alerts the owner to negative reviews, and reports the numbers every month.

**Audience.** One ICP: owner-led home-service shops, HVAC and plumbing first, one or two locations, that log jobs in job software (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or similar). The owner buys; the office manager uses it. Ads and sales pages speak to the owner. Onboarding speaks to the office manager ("nothing new for you to do"). Review requests and replies written for a client speak to that client's customers.

**Positioning.** Rempo runs your Google reviews for you. Every job asked, every review answered. Not another review tool you have to run; not a marketing package with a review tool inside; not one more thing to remember after every job.
