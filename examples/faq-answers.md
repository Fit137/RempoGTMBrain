# Example, FAQ answers

Status: DRAFT, written from the brain on 2026-10-19, pending founder approval.
Audience: the owner. Stage: evaluation. Template: `../templates/faq.md`.

---

**Does it work with my job software?**
We connect to the software you log jobs in, so a request can go out after each completed job. We confirm your system before you buy. If you keep jobs on paper or in a spreadsheet, book a call and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

**What do I have to do?**
Four things, once: connect your job software, give us access to your Google profile, send one team photo, and approve your texting details. After that, we run it.

**What does the setup fee pay for?**
Our team's work in your first week: connecting your software, writing your messages, building your request with your team photo, and registering your texting number with the carriers.

**When do requests start?**
Email requests can start once your software is connected. Texts start once carrier registration clears, which usually takes about a week.

**Will texts annoy my customers?**
Each customer gets one request after the job and at most one reminder. Anyone can reply STOP and they won't hear from us again.

**Is this allowed by Google?**
Yes, the way we run it. Every customer gets the same request. We never screen out unhappy customers, offer rewards, ask customers to name a technician, or tell them what to write. Those are the things Google's rules forbid.

**We already have reviews in Jobber.**
That switch sends a request. Nobody runs it. We run the whole program: a request after every job, one reminder, a reply written for each review, and a monthly report of what went out and what changed.

**Will you guarantee more reviews or a higher ranking?**
No. We control asking every customer and answering every review. How many customers leave one, and where Google ranks you, aren't ours to promise. We track both and show you the numbers every month.

---

## Why this works
- Every answer is two to four sentences and says what happens, not how good it is.
- The hard questions get straight answers, including "no" on guarantees.
- The software answer names no system, because product has not confirmed which connect at launch.
- The timing answer gives the real dependency (carrier registration) instead of a promise; the 7-day guarantee is left out until DECIDED.

## QA
Checked: claims (no promise, no proof points), status (no PLANNED term: no guarantee, no "month to month", no named system), banned words, em dashes, glossary ("job software", "team photo", "setup fee"). Closest to breaking: "Nobody runs it" about the Jobber switch is a scoped competitive claim; it holds for the feature as described in `../rules/do-not-say.md` and is not a claim about Jobber as a company.
