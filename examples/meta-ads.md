# Example, Meta ads: hooks A, B, C and the scorecard lead ad

Status: DRAFT, written from the brain on 2026-10-19, pending founder approval.
Audience: the owner, cold. Stage: awareness. Template: `../templates/meta-ad.md`. Destination: the VSL page, except the lead ad.

---

## Hook A, done for you (9:16 video, 20 seconds)
- **0 to 2 s:** Founder to camera. "We run your Google reviews from your job software."
- **2 to 8 s:** "Does asking for reviews at your shop come down to someone remembering after a long day?"
- **8 to 18 s:** "Once your software is connected, every customer gets a request after the job, then a reminder. Every review gets a reply written for it."
- **18 to 20 s:** "Watch how it works."
- On screen, sound off: "Your reviews, run for you."
- Primary text: "Every job asked, every review answered. You give us access once. We do the rest."
- Headline: "Reviews run for you"

## Hook B, here's what your customer sees (9:16 video, 15 seconds)
- **0 to 2 s:** A phone buzzes. The request image with a first name. Label: "Sample".
- **2 to 6 s:** Voice: "Here's what your customer sees after the job."
- **6 to 13 s:** "Your team's photo. Their first name. Not a generic text. A thank-you from the people who did the work."
- **13 to 15 s:** "Watch how it works."
- On screen: "Here's what your customer sees."
- Primary text: "Requests with your team's photo and their name on them. Set up and run for you."
- Headline: "It shows you asked"

## Hook C, every review answered (9:16 video, 20 seconds)
- **0 to 2 s:** A three-star review appears. Label: "Sample review".
- **2 to 8 s:** "No more staring at a review, wondering what to say."
- **8 to 17 s:** A reply written for that review fills in. "Every review gets a reply written for that review. And every customer gets asked after every job."
- **17 to 20 s:** "Watch how it works."
- On screen: "Every review answered."
- Primary text: "Customers notice templated replies. Ours are written for the review in front of them."
- Headline: "Every review answered"

## Scorecard lead ad (static plus instant form)
- Image: a Google profile card with a "latest review" date circled. Label: "Sample".
- Primary text: "Most customers look for reviews from the last three months (BrightLocal 2026). How do yours look? Take the 2-minute Review Engine Scorecard."
- Headline: "How fresh are your reviews?"
- Form: seven questions, then "We'll email your score and a free Google Review Gap Report." Email consent stated on the form.

---

## Why this works
- Each video carries one idea and one call to action.
- The ads ask or describe; none asserts a fact about the viewer's business.
- Every request and review shown is labeled as a sample; no lift is claimed for the personalized request.
- The one statistic carries its source; the offer line is absent because the offer is not DECIDED.

## QA
Checked: Meta-specific rules (no assertion about the viewer, no competitor names), writing for the ear, banned words, em dashes, claims, status, samples labeled, one call to action each. Closest to breaking: Hook B's "It shows you asked" could imply better results from personalization; it describes the image, not an outcome.
