## Set 1, problem terms (stage: awareness; destination: the scorecard)
Keywords: how to get more google reviews; google review request text; ask customers for google reviews.

Headlines (30 characters or fewer):
1. How Fresh Are Your Reviews?
2. Free Review Engine Scorecard
3. 7 Questions, 2 Minutes
4. See Your Review Gaps
5. Compare With Your Local Top 3
6. Free Google Review Gap Report
7. For Home-Service Shops

Descriptions (90 characters or fewer):
1. Answer 7 questions about how you ask for reviews. Get your score and your three biggest gaps.
2. See how your Google reviews compare with the top three shops near you. Free report by email.

---

## Why this works
- The searcher typed "get more google reviews", and no headline repeats the promise; the ad answers with the process.
- Problem-term ads sell the free scorecard, not the product, matching the stage.
- Every headline reads correctly alone, as responsive ads require.
- No competitor names in the text, no prices, no guarantee, because the offer is not DECIDED.

## QA
Checked: character limits, claims (no review, rating, or ranking promise), status (no PLANNED item; "run from your job software" stays general, no system named), banned words, em dashes, glossary. Closest to breaking: "Every Job Asked" alone could read as unconditional; the descriptions and the page carry the connection condition.
