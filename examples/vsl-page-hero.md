## What you do once. What we do.

| You, once | Rempo, every week |
|---|---|
| Connect your job software | Send a request after every completed job, with your team's photo and the customer's first name |
| Give us access to your Google profile | Send one reminder to customers who haven't reviewed |
| Send one team photo | Write a reply for every new review |
| Approve your texting details | Alert you the day a negative review posts |
| | Show your best recent reviews on your site and in your social feed |
| | Send you a monthly report: requests sent, reviews in, replies posted |

Your office manager has nothing new to run.

## What we don't do

We don't remove reviews, write reviews, screen out unhappy customers, or reward customers for reviews. We never ask a customer to name a technician or tell them what to write. Google's rules forbid all of it, and so do we. We don't promise review counts or rankings either: we ask every customer and answer every review, and we show you the numbers every month.

## Why this works
- The headline is the core claim, verbatim from the copy bank, so it has already passed the claim ledger.
- The two columns make "done for you" concrete and scoped: the owner sees the four things they do.
- The "what we don't do" block turns compliance into a reason to believe instead of fine print.
- The price block is honestly empty rather than filled with prices that are not live.
- Three calls to action in the fixed order: buy, call, scorecard.

## QA
