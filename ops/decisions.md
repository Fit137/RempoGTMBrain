## 2026-10 Channels for the first 90 days (DECIDED, Run 2)
Meta ads (primary), Google Search ads (secondary), and nurture plus retargeting (the middle of the funnel). Cold email, LinkedIn outreach, and LinkedIn Ads are out.

The skill's default channels do not reach an owner-led home-service shop. Owners are mostly not searching for review help, the request is a picture, and a feed is where a picture works. Search captures the owners who are searching and teaches the buyer's words.

**What this does not change:** marketplaces, SEO ranking pages, and partnerships stay on the "later" list; they are not banned.
**Downstream:** `reference/marketing-campaigns.md`, `rules/writing-rules.md` channel discipline.

## 2026-10 Split the GBP manager; drop geotagging; reframe autoposting and the auto-FAQ (DECIDED, Run 2)
The one-time optimization becomes the GBP Tune-Up (order bump). The recurring posting becomes GBP Upkeep, sold as staying current. Geotagging is removed from every asset. The auto-FAQ becomes answers on service pages inside the website.

Evidence sits in the one-time work (primary category, predefined services, completeness). A controlled test found no ranking movement from weekly posts; Google strips location data from uploaded photos; FAQ rich results are limited to government and health sites since 2023-08-08; Business Profile Q&A was removed (API closed 2025-11-03).

**What this does not change:** posting is still sold, as activity. The Review Engine is untouched.
**Downstream:** live page "Google Search Boost" rename (founder); `rules/feature-status.md`.

## 2026-10 The personalized request is the picture, not the claim (DECIDED, Run 2)
Show it in the VSL and ads as what the customer receives. Claim no lift until Rempo's own comparison inside consenting accounts produces one.

No study of personalized images in review requests is on file; survey research shows modest, inconsistent lifts; buyers do not ask for it.

**What this does not change:** it stays a core feature and the strongest creative asset.
**Downstream:** `ops/copy-bank.md` hooks; the request-style test in `reference/experiment-90-day.md`.

## 2026-10 Lead with done for you; core claim set (DECIDED, Run 2)
"Rempo runs your Google reviews for you. Every job asked, every review answered." Done for you is the lead; every job asked, every review answered is its proof; the frictionless purchase is the motion; compliance is a reason to believe; ranking is never a lead.

Done-for-you implementation is the Phase 1 hero (demand 9.4, competitiveness 7.1). Buyers read recency and replies: 74% look for reviews from the last three months, 80% prefer a business that replies to all reviews, 50% are put off by templated replies (BrightLocal 2026).

**What this does not change:** status flags. "Done for you" stays conditional on the software connection.
**Downstream:** `reference/positioning.md`, `ops/copy-bank.md`.

## 2026-10 Claim the process, never a result (DECIDED, standing)
Rempo promises what it controls: asking every customer, following up, replying to every review, reporting. Never review counts, ratings, rankings, calls, or jobs.

Google's review policy, its April 2026 clauses (no staff quotas, no soliciting content naming staff, no supplied wording), and the FTC Consumer Review Rule, with warning letters from 2025-12-22 that reach vendors acting for a business.

**What this does not change:** client numbers are still reported, against a baseline.
**Downstream:** `rules/do-not-say.md`, `reference/review-compliance.md`.

## 2026-10 Self-serve purchase with two sales bumpers (DECIDED, founder)
A VSL at the front, FAQs, a purchase flow, and calls to action: buy (primary), book a 15-minute call, and take the scorecard (opt-in, then nurture). Three or more locations route to a call inside the same funnel.

The founder's preferred path: "Ad, Landing page, Subscribe, Book onboarding, Setup & Activation". The bumpers catch buyers who need a person or are not ready, without a second lane.

**What this does not change:** the funnel is specified, not built (founder, F6).
**Downstream:** `reference/customer-journey.md`, `reference/vsl-funnel-engine.md`.

## 2026-10 One ICP, one funnel, one message (DECIDED, founder plus Run 2)
Owner-led home-service shops in repair and maintenance trades, HVAC and plumbing first, one or two locations, already running jobs in job software. Two maturity stages share the funnel and the message.

The founder fixed one ICP. Run 2 chose it: jobs close daily, the owner decides alone, the website upsell only applies to home services, and the founder's own note makes a CRM a requirement ("They must have existing CRM where they collect client's data").

**What this does not change:** electrical, garage door, and appliance repair follow once proof exists, with the same message.
**Downstream:** `reference/icp-personas.md`, checkout qualifier.

## 2026-10 Service in front, product behind, one funnel (DECIDED, founder)
Rempo is a service-dominated hybrid. The done-for-you service is the front-end offer; product add-ons are sold later to service clients only, never lead an ad, and leave "Hold" only under the release rule (asked for by two clients unprompted, visible in their data, delivered done-for-you, a support playbook with a time cap, priced as an add-on).

The service is what the evidence supports, what competitors leave open, and what can be sold now at a price that pays for advertising. The founder wants to limit support load.

**What this does not change:** the AI voice receptionist is the first add-on, offered when call data shows missed calls.
**Downstream:** `reference/offer-architecture.md`, `rules/feature-status.md` HELD items.

## 2026-10 Recommended offer and pricing (OPEN, awaiting the founder)
Recommendation: one plan card, the Review Engine at $197 per location per month plus $299 setup; GBP Tune-Up $197 order bump; GBP Upkeep $97/month one-click upsell; website $1,497 plus $49/month on a call; AI voice receptionist $97/month; annual $1,970 per location at month 3; three or more locations quoted on a call; "24/7 Support" removed.

Per location scored 8.9 of 10 as the value metric. $97 to $149 rejected for the done-for-you offer: payback at a $350 CAC is 3.6 months at $97 against 1.8 at $197.

**What this does not change:** until DECIDED, the live page's prices are the only prices a buyer may be shown.
**Downstream once DECIDED:** the live pricing page, every buy button, `rules/feature-status.md`.

## 2026-10 Paid start with a 7-day process guarantee, replacing the free trial (OPEN, awaiting the founder and counsel)
Recommendation: "First requests out within 7 days of your software connection, or your setup fee back. Month to month." The trial returns only if checkout conversion is the measured bottleneck.

A trial means setup labor before payment, a "free" claim that needs the fee beside it (16 CFR Part 251), and texting registration that can use half the trial.

**What this does not change:** the live trial stays live until the page changes, with the fee disclosed.
**Downstream once DECIDED:** offer line in `ops/copy-bank.md`, checkout, FAQ.
