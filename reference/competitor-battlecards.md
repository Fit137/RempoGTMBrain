# Competitor battlecards

Load for: sales calls, comparison pages, FAQ answers, and search ads that meet a rival. Distills: Phase 2 (`dashboards/02-competitive-market-analysis.md`) and research Step 0D, section 5. Synthesized. Facts dated 2026-10; re-check before citing, and name the source on any comparison page.

Each card leads with the rival's real strength. That is what makes it credible inside the team and on a call.

## NiceJob
- **Real strength:** easy setup, runs in the background, integrates with Jobber and Housecall Pro, $75 to $125/month bought without a call, a 14-day trial without a card.
- **Real gap:** the owner runs it. Reporting is basic; timing and wording are restrictive; onboarding help is slow (G2 and Capterra themes).
- **How we win:** effort. Rempo sets it up and runs it, writes a reply for each review, and shows the numbers monthly.
- **Say out loud:**
  1. "NiceJob is a good tool, and it's cheaper. You run it."
  2. "Rempo is a service that runs it: setup, requests, replies, the monthly report."
  3. "If you want to run it yourself, a tool is the cheaper choice, and I'd tell you that."

## Jobber Reviews (and the Housecall Pro review feature)
- **Real strength:** already inside the software where the job closes. The request fires on job close, visit complete, or invoice paid, with no second tool.
- **Real gap:** Jobber users only; marketing automation sits on the $199/month Grow plan; follow-up is fixed; nobody owns the program once the switch is on. Replies and reporting stay with the shop.
- **How we win:** a program someone runs, not a switch nobody watches. Personalized requests, a reply written for each review, a monthly report.
- **Say out loud:**
  1. "Keep Jobber. We connect to it." (Only once product confirms the connection; until then, "we check your software before you buy".)
  2. "The switch sends a request. Someone still has to own replies, follow-ups, and the numbers."
  3. "We make sure the same customer is not asked twice." (`{DEFERRED: product}`, requirement 18)

## Podium
- **Real strength:** the name owners know; breadth across chat, text, calls, and reviews; G2 rating about 4.6 from 2,000+ reviews.
- **Real gap:** sold by demo; pricing unpublished (reported about $399 to $599/month on annual terms, third-party); cancellation is the top complaint (100% negative across 24 Capterra mentions). Its own positioning has moved to "the #1 converting AI Employee" (Podium's claim), with reviews one feature of a larger sale.
- **How we win:** one job done well, a published price, bought online. Check the buyer's contract terms before suggesting a switch.
- **Say out loud:**
  1. "Podium does a lot. If you only want your reviews handled, you're paying for the rest."
  2. "Our price is on the page. You don't need a demo to buy." (once the offer is DECIDED, add "month to month")
  3. "If you're under contract, finish it. We'll be here."

## Footbridge Media
- **Real strength:** one price, $249/month, for a contractor website, hosting, SEO, citations, profile optimization, and review management (Birdeye); no contract, no build fee; 90-day money-back guarantee; price fixed for three years. The sharpest comparison for a service sold online.
- **Real gap:** reviews are a bundled third-party tool inside a website program, not a review program run from the client's job data. Independent reviews are scarce (none on G2).
- **How we win:** Rempo runs one thing, your Google reviews, from your own job data, and adds the profile or website later if you want them.
- **Say out loud:**
  1. "If you need a new website and the basics in one bill, Footbridge is a fair deal."
  2. "If your site is fine and your reviews are the gap, we run that program for you."
  3. "You can add your profile or website with us later, in the same account."

## Google Business Profile by hand
- **Real strength:** free and native: a review link, a QR code, replies once verified.
- **Real gap:** manual; no follow-up; depends on someone remembering on a busy day.
- **How we win:** every completed job asked, a reminder, a reply for each review, without anyone at the shop sending anything.
- **Say out loud:**
  1. "The link is free. Remembering to send it after every job is the hard part."
  2. "We send it for you, after every completed job, with one reminder."
  3. "And every review gets a reply written for it."
