# Customer journey: the one funnel

Load for: writing to a funnel stage, choosing the call to action and bumper, and onboarding copy. Distills: Phase 7, `dashboards/07-customer-journey.md`. The funnel is specified, not built.

Self-serve first: a buyer can go from an ad to a paid account without speaking to anyone. Bumpers catch the buyers who need a person or are not ready, inside the same funnel.

## The funnel at a glance

## The seven stages

| Stage | Mindset | Questions they ask | Assets | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Problem awareness | "The shop down the road has newer reviews than we do." | Why do they have more? Does it matter? | Personalized-request video ad; scorecard lead ad; search ads on review terms | CTR; scorecard opt-ins |
| 2. Solution discovery | "There has to be a way without me remembering." | Can my job software do it? A tool? An agency? | VSL page; scorecard and Gap Report; comparison pages later | Play rate; scorecard to VSL |
| 3. Vendor evaluation | "Will this work with what we use, and what does it cost?" | Jobber or Housecall Pro? What do I do? Setup fee? Cancel? Texts annoying? Allowed by Google? | FAQ; price and guarantee on one screen; sample request to their phone; call bumper | Clicks on "Get started"; calls booked |
| 4. Purchase | "Let me get it started and see it work." | When do requests start? Who sets it up? | Checkout, bump, upsell, thank-you, onboarding call | Checkout conversion; days to first request |
| 5. Value realization | "Is anything happening?" | Are customers responding? Are replies going out? | First requests and replies; weekly summary (`{DEFERRED: product}`); monthly report; alerts | Ask and reply coverage; reviews vs baseline |
| 6. Expansion and advocacy | "What else can you take off my plate?" | Our profile? Our old website? Missed calls? A friend's shop? | 30-day review; Upkeep, website, receptionist; referral offer never tied to reviews; case-study permission request | Attach rate; permissions granted |
| 7. Renewal | "Is it still worth it now our reviews look good?" | What if we stop? What did it do this quarter? | Monthly report; quarterly Gap Report refresh; annual offer at month 3 | Retention at months 2, 6, 12 |

## What decides success

- **Critical:** days to first request; the software connection; the office manager's first week (nothing new to do); a monthly report that makes invisible work visible.
- **Blockers:** buyers without a CRM; texting registration eating the first week; founder capacity beside a full-time job; Level 2 buyers under contract.
- **Accelerators:** the scorecard as opt-in and quarterly renewal touch; the sample request to the buyer's own phone; app marketplaces once connections exist; the annual plan at month 3.
