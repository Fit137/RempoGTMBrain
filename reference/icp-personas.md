# ICP and personas

Load for: targeting, who a piece of copy speaks to, buyer blockers, and the founding cohort. Distills: Phases 4 and 5, `dashboards/04-icp.md` and `dashboards/05-buying-committee.md`.

## One ICP

**Owner-led home-service businesses in repair and maintenance trades, starting with residential HVAC and plumbing, with one or two locations, that already run jobs in field-service software or a CRM holding customer phone numbers or emails.** United States, English (default; open decision).

- **Home services:** the website upsell only applies here; 91% of homeowners rate reviews important when choosing an HVAC contractor (ACHR News 2024); Local Services Ads rank on rating and number of reviews (Google).
- **Repair and maintenance first:** jobs close every day, so there is someone to ask every day. Repair and maintenance pages convert almost three times better than home and renovation pages (Unbounce 2024).
- **HVAC and plumbing first:** about 120,000 to 170,000 HVAC businesses (IBISWorld; Orbital) and about 127,000 plumbing businesses (IBISWorld); average jobs $350 and $338 (Angi). Electrical, garage door, and appliance repair follow once proof exists, same message.
- **The CRM is part of the ICP:** the founder's note, "They must have existing CRM where they collect client's data." Without job records, "after every job" cannot run without someone at the shop.
- **One or two locations:** the owner decides alone and pays by card. Three or more go to a call inside the same funnel.

## Two maturity levels, one funnel

| | Level 1: no review system | Level 2: tried a tool and stalled |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | HVAC and plumbing, 1 to 2 locations, 3 to 25 staff, 2+ years trading, 5+ completed jobs a week (floor to validate) | Same, plus electrical, garage door, appliance repair once proof exists |
| Pain | Asking depends on whoever remembers; the newest review is weeks or months old; replies unwritten; a nearby competitor looks fresher | The switch in the job software was turned on once and nobody owns it; generic texts; templated or missing replies; a contract they cannot leave |
| What wins | Done for them, from software they already use; every job asked, every review answered; bought online | The same, plus a program someone runs and replies that sound like the shop |
| Triggers | A bad review; a slow week; a new competitor; a new office manager; starting Local Services Ads | A tool contract ending; a price increase; a run of reviews with no reply; switching job software |

## Roles

| | Owner (decision maker) | Office manager (user and champion) | Manager (often absent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Influence | Final; holds the card | Low on purchase; high on keep or cancel after month one | Medium |
| Blocks with | Price against NiceJob or the job-software switch; the setup fee before seeing anything; "we tried this before"; not wanting a call; a contract with notice periods | "One more system for me"; texting customers too much; reviews naming them; double requests (Level 2) | Reviews used to judge technicians; an outside vendor with something similar to sell |
| Wants | Fresh reviews next to the competitor; less admin; pride in the work | Nothing new to do; no awkward asking | A profile that reflects the team's work |
| Assets that win them | VSL, FAQ, price and guarantee on one screen, scorecard result, breakeven on their own ticket size | "What you do, what we do", a sample request to their phone, the onboarding checklist | Sample monthly report; a one-page "what changes, what stays" |
| Where to find | Meta (Facebook, Instagram), Google Search, YouTube, trade Facebook groups | Inside onboarding | Inside onboarding |

**The rule:** ads and the VSL speak to the owner; onboarding is built for the office manager, whose first experience must be "nothing new for me to do". That makes the software connection the most important onboarding step.

The failure rate per question is a 90-day experiment metric.

## Founding cohort (the first 5 to 10 paying shops)

- **Profile:** busy HVAC or plumbing shop; owner reachable by phone; jobs in Jobber or Housecall Pro; 3 to 25 staff; 5+ jobs a week; willing to compare personalized and plain requests.
- **They get:** founding pricing or a waived setup fee (`{TBD}`), direct access to the founder, influence on the product.
- **They give:** a baseline at signup, feedback calls in weeks 2 and 6, consent to the request-style comparison.
- **Never asked for:** a testimonial or a review of Rempo as a condition of terms. Any testimonial is voluntary, used only in exact permitted wording, and discloses the founding relationship.
