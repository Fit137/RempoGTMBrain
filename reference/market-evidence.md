# Market evidence

Load for: any statistic in copy, and checking whether a claim has evidence behind it. Distills: research Step 0D, `research/step-0d-service-outcomes.md` (2026-10-19), copied to `sources/raw/research/`. Findings came from search summaries of the named sources (direct fetches were blocked), so cite the named source, not the summary.

Quality labels: **Primary** (Google, the FTC, peer-reviewed or primary research), **Survey** (stated sample), **Expert survey** (practitioner opinion), **Vendor** (vendor data, agency guides, single case studies: directional only, never a headline).

## Evidence by claim (what the research supports)

| Claim | Strength |
|---|---|
| A consistent ask produces reviews | Strong |
| Any specific request-to-review rate | Weak: every rate is vendor data (for example, about 15 reviews per 100 email requests, GatherUp's own data) |
| Recency, rating, and replies shape whether a searcher calls | Strong |
| Reviews move rankings | Moderate: they feed prominence, but proximity and primary category dominate (Whitespark 2026, expert survey) |
| Replies move rankings | Weak: no controlled study found it |
| Personalized images raise response | Weak: no study of images in review requests; postal-survey personalization odds ratio 1.45 in one pooled analysis, no consistent effect in a 39,518-person Dutch experiment |
| One-time profile optimization helps rank | Strong (primary category, predefined services, completeness) |
| Weekly posting helps rank | Not supported (controlled test, no movement across 441 keywords) |
| Geotagged photos help rank | Not supported (Google strips the data on upload) |
| An FAQ widget earns search features | Not supported since 2023-08-08 (FAQ rich results limited to government and health sites) |
| A page per service helps local organic | Moderate (expert survey) |

## Never use as a claim

- Any request-to-review rate, SMS-vs-email lift, or reminder lift: vendor data, and timing evidence conflicts.
- NiceJob's "+690% reviews" tree-service case: a single vendor case study.
- Spiegel's "+270% purchase likelihood with five reviews": e-commerce data, not local services.
- The 3-pack median of about 47 reviews (Local Falcon): a vendor study; useful for the Gap Report context, not as a target.
- Any number about Rempo's own results: none exist.

## Not found

Any independent study of a done-for-you review service against a self-run tool; any study of personalized images in review requests; adoption of field-service software among small US home-service shops. Rempo's own data is the only route to these.
