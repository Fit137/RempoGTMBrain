# Marketing campaigns and asset backlog

Load for: which campaigns run, which assets exist or are planned, who makes them, and when. Distills: Phase 8, `dashboards/08-assets-library.md`. Every asset below is PLANNED: specified for a brief, not built.

## The channel decision

| # | Campaign | Role in the one funnel | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meta ads (Facebook, Instagram) | Primary: creates demand, sends it to the VSL page or the scorecard | Owners are mostly not searching for review help; the request is a picture; lead-form ads deliver the scorecard without a landing page |
| 2 | Google Search ads | Secondary: captures owners already searching | Small budget; teaches the words buyers use |
| 3 | Nurture and retargeting | The middle of the funnel: opt-ins and VSL visitors who did not buy | Turns traffic that is not ready into a list Rempo owns |

**Out:** cold email, LinkedIn outreach, LinkedIn Ads, cold calls or texts to prospects. They do not reach an owner-led home-service shop. **Later, not in the 90 days:** field-service app marketplaces once connections exist; SEO comparison pages ranked; partnerships with trade groups and web designers.

## The hire (OPEN, recommended)

Media buyer plus research strategist at $900/month, not media buyer plus creative agency at $2,400/month: over 90 days the agency costs $7,200, 48% of the $15,000. The creative this funnel needs is modest and mostly founder-made: one VSL (founder records, freelancer edits), one lead message with three hooks in two formats refreshed every four weeks (about 18 assets), and the product itself as the strongest creative. Revisit the agency at day 60 only if a hook is proven and creative fatigue is the limit.
