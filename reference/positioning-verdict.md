## Fork 1. Lead with a service or with software? Settled: service in front

| Dimension | Done-for-you service | SaaS |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Easy: revenue from the first client, on software that already exists | Hard: the product must exist and win users first |
| Scale | Hard: each account adds labor | Easy: the next account costs little |
| Sell | Easy: the buyer pays for work done | Hard: the buyer pays for a capability they must operate |
| Who does the work | The vendor | The client |
| Outcome certainty | High: does not depend on the buyer finding time | Lower: depends on the buyer using the tool |

The service is what the evidence supports (Phase 0), what competitors leave open (Phase 2), and what can be sold now at a price that pays for advertising (Phase 9). The product layer is where margin can grow later. Product add-ons sit behind the service in the same funnel, under the release rule in [offer architecture](offer-architecture.md).

**Not reopened unless:** hours per account fall far enough that one assistant runs many more accounts, clients ask to run it themselves, or agencies ask to resell the program.

## Fork 2. One ICP or two motions? Settled: one ICP, one funnel, one message

Run 1 (2026-10-16) recommended two motions split by number of locations. The founder fixed one ICP, one funnel, one message for Run 2, and Run 1's split is superseded. Shops with three or more locations reach a "Book a call" bumper inside the same funnel; there is no separate sales-led lane and no group buyer. Running a service and a product as separate go-to-markets doubles the team, the ICP, and the marketing.

## Fork 3. Sales-led or self-serve? Settled: self-serve with sales bumpers

The founder's preferred path: "Ad, Landing page, Subscribe, Book onboarding, Setup & Activation". Every Rempo service is a one-decision-maker purchase under 30 days. B2B buyers increasingly prefer rep-free buying: 67% in 2025 (Gartner, survey of 646). Bumpers catch the buyers who need a person ("Book a 15-minute call") or are not ready ("Take the scorecard"). The 90-day experiment measures the self-serve share; if it falls below half its floor at day 60 while call-to-purchase is green, "Book a call" becomes the primary call to action until the page is reworked.

## Fork 4. Lead with which angle? Settled: done for you

Done for you leads; every job asked, every review answered proves it; the personalized request is the picture; the frictionless purchase is the motion; ranking is never a lead. Detail: [positioning](positioning.md).

## Fork 5. Price at $97 to $149? Settled: no, for the done-for-you offer

The founder first considered $97 to $149/month. At the $350 working CAC, months of subscription revenue to recover it: $97 gives 3.6, $149 gives 2.3, $197 gives 1.8. Setup labor and the onboarding call cost the same at any price, and at $97 to $149 Rempo would sit at NiceJob's price ($75 to $125) while doing the work. A lower entry, if ever needed, is a different product (software the owner runs), sold later through the release rule.

## Fork 6. Free trial or paid start? OPEN

**Recommendation:** replace the live 15-Day Free Trial with a paid start and a process guarantee: "First requests out within 7 days of your software connection, or your setup fee back." Terms with counsel.

**Why:** a trial means setup labor before payment, a "free" claim that needs the setup fee disclosed beside it (FTC guide, 16 CFR Part 251), and texting registration (A2P 10DLC, about a week, vendor estimate) that can use half the trial. A paid start removes all three and matches the founder's preferred path.

**Until decided:** the live trial stays as the page shows it, with the fee beside any "free". The trial returns as a test (from day 60) only if checkout conversion is the measured bottleneck and abandoned-checkout replies name price or the trial.
