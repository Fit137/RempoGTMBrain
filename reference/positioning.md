# Positioning

Load for: what Rempo leads on, the core claim, and the statements against each kind of alternative. Distills: Phase 3, `dashboards/03-positioning.md`, and Step 2.16.

**Category:** done-for-you Google review management for local service businesses.
**Niche:** owner-led home-service shops that already keep customer records in job software.

## Core claim

**Rempo runs your Google reviews for you. Every job asked, every review answered.**

Supporting line for the VSL and checkout: connected to the job software you already use, live within a week, month to month. ("Live within a week" and "month to month" are PLANNED; see [`rules/feature-status.md`](../rules/feature-status.md).)

## Positioning statements

**Against review software (NiceJob, Podium, the switch in your job software):**
Not another review tool you have to run. Rempo connects to the software you already log jobs in and runs your review program for you: every job asked, every review answered with a reply written for it. You buy it online, at a published price.

**Against agencies and bundles (Footbridge Media and similar):**
Not a marketing package with a review tool inside. Rempo runs one thing, your Google reviews, from your own job data, and shows you the numbers every month: requests sent, reviews in, replies posted. Add your profile or your website later if you want them.

**Against the status quo (asking by hand, or not asking):**
Not one more thing to remember after every job. Rempo asks every customer after every job and follows up, so asking no longer depends on anyone having a spare minute that day.

Each statement was checked against the claim rules: no "only", no result claimed, no ranking promised, no staff blamed, and "done for you" conditional on the software connection. ("No contract" is held back until the offer is DECIDED.)

## What every VSL, page, or video must carry

1. **Lead with the service.** The first line says Rempo runs it for you, from the software they already use.
2. **Show the request and a reply.** A personalized request built with Rempo's own team, and a reply to a real-looking review, both labeled as samples.
3. **Say what the owner does once:** connect the job software, give profile access, send a team photo. Short and true.
4. **Price, setup fee, and guarantee on one screen,** next to the buy button (the Run 2 offer only once DECIDED).
5. **Answer the Level 2 objection** ("we already have reviews in Jobber") in the FAQ.
6. **Three calls to action,** in this order: buy, book a call, take the scorecard.
7. **No ranking promise, no review counts, no geotagging, no request copy that asks customers to name staff.**
