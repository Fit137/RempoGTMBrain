## Setup fee and the start (PLANNED)

$299 on month to month, charged at purchase: it pays for setup labor and filters buyers who are not ready. Waived on annual. Refunded if first requests are not out within 7 days of the software connection; the clock starts at connection because the connection depends on the client.

## ROI anchors (arithmetic for the buyer to check, never a promise of jobs)

- **Year-one cost:** Review Engine $2,663 ($2,364 plus $299 setup); with GBP Upkeep $3,827.
- **One job covers:** a $338 plumbing job (Angi) covers 1.7 months of the Review Engine; a $350 HVAC repair (Angi) covers 1.8. With Upkeep: 1.1 and 1.2.
- **Breakeven:** about 7.9 plumbing jobs or 7.6 HVAC repairs a year in year one; one extra average job about every 6.6 weeks.
- **Asking by hand:** 3 office hours a week costs about $2,850 a year at the BLS median receptionist wage ($18.27/hour, May 2025).
- **Alternatives:** Podium about $399/month (third-party, annual terms); done-for-you review services $500 to $2,500/month (agency guides); NiceJob $75 to $125/month, cheaper because the owner runs it. Say that plainly.

Approved message: "One average plumbing job covers more than a month and a half." Breakeven arithmetic on the buyer's own ticket size, never a promise of new jobs.
