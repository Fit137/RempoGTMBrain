# Review compliance

Load for: any copy about reviews, and every piece of delivery copy written for a client (review requests, reminders, replies, social posts from reviews, the website display). Distills: `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5 and research Step 0D, section 1e. Not legal advice: counsel reviews the guarantee terms and text consent wording.

Rempo writes and sends requests on a client's behalf, so it is a third party acting for the business. The FTC's position is that a business can be liable for what third parties do for it, which puts Rempo inside the rule, not beside it.

## 1. Google's review policies (Maps rating manipulation)

**What they say.** No review gating (asking only happy customers), no incentives for reviews, no fake or bought reviews. On 2026-04-17 Google added two clauses: a business may not ask staff to solicit a set number of reviews, and may not solicit reviews with specific content, "including content that identifies a staff member" (secondary reports of the change; check the live policy page). Google can pause reviews on a profile when it sees unusual activity (2026-04-16, Trust and Safety report).

**For sales copy:** never promise counts or ratings; never sell a satisfaction filter; "Every customer asked the same way, under Google's rules" is the approved line. No technician leaderboards, quotas, or contests as a feature or a tip.

**For delivery copy:**
- The same Google request to every customer with a completed job and contact details.
- Ask for an honest review. Nothing about rating, wording, or which technician to mention.
- No discount, entry, or gift for a review. Referral rewards, if ever offered, are never tied to a review.
- The team photo in a personalized request is allowed. Asking the customer to name the person in it is not.
- A negative-review alert is fine; an internal survey that routes only happy customers to Google is gating.

## 2. FTC Consumer Review Rule (16 CFR Part 465)

**What it says.** In effect since 2024-10-21. Bans fake reviews and testimonials, buying reviews, review suppression, and misrepresenting that displayed reviews are all or most of a business's reviews. First public enforcement: warning letters to 10 companies on 2025-12-22, civil penalties up to $53,088 per violation (law-firm summaries).

**For sales copy:** no testimonial, quote, or result that does not exist. The founding cohort is never asked for a testimonial as a condition of its terms; any voluntary one discloses the relationship.

**For delivery copy and the website display:** the widget shows recent reviews from Google and never states or implies it shows every review. Social posts quote reviewers by first name only and need approval (`{DEFERRED: product}`). Rempo never writes a review for a client or its customers.

## 3. The FTC guide on the word "free" (16 CFR Part 251)

**What it says.** A "free" offer must disclose its conditions clearly at the outset, close to the word "free".

**For sales copy:** while the live 15-Day Free Trial stands, any "free" sits beside the $299 setup fee and when it is charged. If the paid start is DECIDED, no "free" anywhere except the scorecard and the Gap Report, which are free with an opt-in.

## 4. Texting: A2P 10DLC registration and consent

**What it means.** Business texts from a local number need brand and campaign registration (A2P 10DLC). Registration takes about a week (vendor estimate) and has one-time and monthly fees (third-party, check current rates). Customers must have given their number with consent to be texted.

**For sales copy:** "Email requests can start right away. Texts start once your carrier registration clears." Never promise texts on day one.

**For delivery copy:** text only customers with consent captured where they gave their number (wording `{TBD}` with counsel); include an opt-out in every text; one request and one reminder, no more. Requirement 6 in [product requirements](product-requirements.md).

## 5. Search and star snippets

Since September 2019 Google shows no star snippets for a business's own reviews on its own site, including through widgets. Never say the widget puts stars in Google.

## 6. AI voice receptionist (when released)

Outbound AI calls need a consent design before sale (requirement 23). Inbound answering has no such claim attached.
