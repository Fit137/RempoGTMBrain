# Do not say

The claim hygiene spine. Check every claim here before it ships. Left column is forbidden or risky. Right column is the safe replacement. Every review claim sits under Google's review policies (including the April 2026 clauses) and the FTC Consumer Review Rule (16 CFR Part 465), which reaches vendors acting for a business, so it reaches Rempo.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-19

## Hard bans

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Get more reviews", "X new reviews a month", any review guarantee | "We ask every customer after every job, and follow up." Report the client's numbers against their baseline. |
| "Rank higher on Google", "better ranking" | "Reviews are one of the things Google weighs in local results. We track your position, and never promise it." |
| "Personalized requests get more reviews", any lift figure | "Your customer gets a request with your team's photo and their first name, not a generic text." |
| Any customer count, result, testimonial, quote, logo, case study | `[PROOF POINT NEEDED]`. The proof set is empty. |
| A statistic with no source | The same statistic with its source in the sentence, or cut it. |
| Run 2 prices, the paid start, or the 7-day guarantee as live | Omit, or label the draft `[OFFER PENDING]` until `ops/decisions.md` marks the offer DECIDED. |
| "Start Free" with the setup fee out of sight | The fee beside the word "free", or no "free" at all (16 CFR Part 251). |
| "24/7 Support" | Support hours as staffed, `{TBD}`. |
| "Every review gets an instant AI reply" | "Every review gets a reply written for that review." |
| "No software to learn, no buttons to click", "autopilot" | "We connect to the software you already log jobs in. After that, requests go out without anyone at the shop sending them." |
| "Done for you" with no scope | "We set it up and run it. You give us access once": software connection, Google access, a team photo, texting details. |
| "Works with Jobber / Housecall Pro" before product confirms it | "We connect to your job software. We confirm yours before you buy." |
| "Catch unhappy customers before they post" | "Every customer gets the same request. Alerts tell you when a review posts." |
| "Shows all your reviews", "stars in Google" for the widget | "Recent reviews from Google, on your site." |
| "Posting weekly boosts your ranking", "geotagged photos help you rank" | "Your profile and social stay current with your real work." Geotagging: nothing. |
| "Your FAQ shows up in Google" | "Answers to what your customers ask, on your service pages." |
| "We remove bad reviews", "reputation management" alone | Name what is included. "We do not remove reviews." |
| Any product-layer item (inbox, booking, chat, pipeline) in an ad or headline | Nothing. HELD items are never advertised. |
| Copy for cold email, LinkedIn, or cold texts to prospects | "That channel is out of scope for this GTM." Offer a Meta ad, a search ad, or a nurture email instead. |

## In delivery copy (requests and replies written for a client)
| Do not write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| "Mention Mike, your technician!" or any staff name request | "Would you share an honest review of your visit?" |
| Suggested wording, star prompts ("leave us 5 stars") | Nothing about content or rating. |
| Discounts, entries, or gifts for a review | Nothing. Referral rewards, if any, never tied to a review. |
| A satisfaction check that routes only happy customers to Google | The same Google request to every customer. |
| A reply that names the customer's address, job details they did not mention, or argues | A reply to what the reviewer said, calm, first name at most. |
| Technician quotas, leaderboards, contests | Nothing. |

## In video and the VSL
Nothing PARTIAL, PLANNED, or HELD is shown working. Samples are labeled on screen. Every spoken statistic appears on screen with its source. No caveat dropped for pacing: "once your software is connected" stays in.

## In ads
Ask or describe; never assert a fact about the viewer ("Your reviews are stale"). Competitor names only as search keywords, never in ad text. The offer line in primary text only once the offer is DECIDED.

## Claims that need scoping (verify before print)
- **"The only done-for-you review service."** Counter-example: Footbridge Media bundles review management at $249/month; agencies on GoHighLevel sell the same features; done-for-you review services run $500/month and up. Safe form: "Rempo runs your review program for you, from the job software you already use, at a published price you can buy online."
- **"None of the tools runs the program for you."** Holds for the named set (NiceJob, the Jobber review feature, Podium) as of 2026-10: each is software the shop operates. Name the tool and date the comparison.
- **"Cheaper than Podium."** Podium does not publish prices; about $399/month on annual terms is a third-party report. Safe form: "Podium is reported to cost about $399 a month on annual terms" with the source and date, on comparison pages only.
- **"Live within a week."** Depends on the client's software connection and texting registration. Safe form, once DECIDED: "First requests within 7 days of your software connection." Email can start before texts.
- **"Every customer asked."** Holds once the job software sends completed jobs; scope it: "every completed job with a phone number or email".
- **"Every review answered."** Holds as a process; approval mode is unconfirmed. Do not add "instantly".
