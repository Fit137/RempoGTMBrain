# Writing rules

These apply to every word of output, no exceptions.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-19

## Mechanics
- No em dashes anywhere. Use commas, or restructure the sentence.
- Banned words: leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, empower, elevate, revolutionize, game-changing, best-in-class, world-class, supercharge, turbocharge, harness, delve, tapestry, realm, testament, foster, facilitate, utilize, myriad, plethora, pivotal.
- Banned structures: "It's not just X, it's Y"; "In today's fast-paced world"; "Whether you're"; a rhetorical question as the opener of a page or email; decorative rules of three.
- Short paragraphs, one to three sentences. One idea per sentence. Front-load the point.
- Specific numbers, named steps, and verifiable facts over adjectives. No stacked adjectives.
- Every statistic carries its source in the same sentence: "(BrightLocal 2026)".
- Every sample request, reply, report, or screen is labeled as a sample.

## Tone
Plain, direct, and respectful of the trade. The buyer is a busy owner who runs crews and answers phones, and has heard every marketing promise. Write like someone who does the work: what happens, who does it, what the owner does once, what it costs. Admit the trade-off when there is one: a tool is cheaper if you want to run it yourself.

## Writing for the ear (VSL, video ads, sales calls)
- Short spoken sentences. One clause where you would normally write two.
- Contractions and second person. Talk to one owner, not an audience.
- Front-load the point of each sentence. Listeners cannot re-read.
- Signpost out loud ("Here's what you do once."), not with headers.
- Read every script aloud before it ships. If you stumble, cut it.
- Every statistic said aloud is also on screen with its source.

## Claim discipline
Every "only", "first", "best", "never", and "no other" is checked against `do-not-say.md` before it ships. Every capability is checked against `feature-status.md`: anything PLANNED or HELD is "coming soon" or omitted, in the same sentence it appears. A claim true only in one condition names the condition: "once your job software is connected". Describe the process Rempo controls, never a result it does not.

## Audience discipline
One ICP. Ads, the VSL, and the page speak to the owner. Onboarding, in-account emails, and the monthly report speak to the office manager. Delivery copy (requests and replies written for a client) speaks to that client's customers and follows `reference/review-compliance.md`. The product layer never leads.

## Channel discipline
This GTM writes: Meta ads, Google Search ads, the VSL and VSL page, the FAQ, checkout and upsell pages, comparison pages, the scorecard and Gap Report, nurture email to people who opted in, retargeting, the sales call guide, onboarding, and delivery copy for clients.
It refuses: cold email, LinkedIn outreach, LinkedIn Ads, cold calls or texts to prospects. Redirect the request to the nearest channel that is in.

## Signature lines (approved, use them)
- Rempo runs your Google reviews for you. Every job asked, every review answered.
- Every customer asked, every review answered, and you never have to run it.
- Not another review tool you have to run.
- Not one more thing to remember after every job.
- You give us access once. We do the rest.
- Here's what your customer sees.

## Formatting
Prefer prose. Lists only when the content is a list. Headlines short and concrete. One primary call to action per section, the same one at the top and bottom of a page.
