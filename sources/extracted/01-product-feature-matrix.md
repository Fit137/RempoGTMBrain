| What the matrix says |
| 1.  The hero is still the service. Done-for-you implementation is the strongest point on the plot. The features inside the front-end offer are table stakes; the service wrapped around them is what the buyer pays for. |
| 2.  Two borderline heroes have clear places in the funnel. GBP optimization becomes the checkout order bump; the AI voice receptionist becomes the first product add-on. |
| 3.  The differentiators are real and quiet. The personalized request, social posts from reviews, and video testimonials score high on competitiveness and low on stated demand. They belong in the VSL and the onboarding, shown rather than claimed. |
| 4.  The product layer is mostly commodities, which supports the founder's instinct to keep it small to limit support. Phase 11 sets the rule for when an item leaves "Hold". |
| 5.  Geotagging leaves the offer. It scores low on demand once buyers are asked, and Phase 0 found no lasting effect. |

| Step 1.6. Demand driver |
| Attribute | Description |
| What it is | The Review Engine Scorecard, the middle-of-funnel asset. Seven questions in about two minutes, an instant score, and a results page that shows the business how fresh its reviews look to a buyer: reviews in the last 90 days, the latest review date, reply coverage, and the customers finished last m... |
| Immediate value | The score and the three biggest gaps on screen, without a call. 74% of consumers look for reviews from the last three months (BrightLocal 2026), so recency is a gap the owner can check against their own profile |
| How it helps in problem identification | It turns "we should get more reviews" into a number: customers finished each week who are never asked, and how many weeks since the last review |
| How it exposes the need to use Rempo | Every gap traces to the same habit: ask every customer after every job, follow up, reply to every review. Rempo runs that habit from the CRM they already use |
| ICP fit | Asks which job software they use and how many jobs they complete each week, the two facts that decide whether the service will work for them |
| Conversion lever | The results page routes by fit: ready buyers to the VSL page and checkout; buyers with questions or 3+ locations to a call; everyone else into a nurture sequence |
| Top-of-funnel use case | Meta lead ads and retargeting ("How fresh are your Google reviews?"), Google Search for problem terms, and the exit point on the VSL page for visitors who are not ready to buy |
| Delivery starts manual for the Gap Report: the assistant builds it from public Google results within one business day. Automating it through Google's official APIs is {DEFERRED: product}. |

| Google Review Gap Report | High | Medium | Light tool | The business against its local top three on review count, last-90-day reviews, rating, and reply coverage | Offered on the scorecard results page and in the nurture sequence |
