| Step 2.15. Category summary |
| Vector 1: Easy, hands-off automation Leader: NiceJob, with Jobber close behind for its own users. Impact: wins self-serve buyers through low friction and integrations, and sets the tool price at $75 to $125 a month. |
| Vector 2: Platform breadth Leader: Podium among software; Footbridge among services. Impact: Podium drives demo-led sales on annual contracts; Footbridge wins buyers who want one bill for the site and the marketing. Breadth is a cost for a buyer who only wants reviews handled. |
| Vector 3: Done for you, from your CRM Leader: Rempo, by design. Footbridge delivers done-for-you marketing; its reviews run on a bundled tool. Impact: brings the buyer's effort close to zero and justifies a price above the tools. It turns setup into part of the product rather than a cost of sale. |
| Vector 4: Personal, not templated Leader: Rempo, by design. Impact: the one claim in the category that shows in a single image (the request) and a single reply. Its effect on response rates is unproven, {TBD} until Phase 10 measures it. |
