Run after Phases 4 and 5. Run 1 compared the founder's three angles and chose done-for-you as the lead. Run 2 keeps that call and changes what proves it, because the research in Phase 0 moved: what buyers read on a profile (recent reviews, replies) has stronger evidence than the personalized request or rankings.


## The angles, compared again with Run 2's evidence

Core claim: Rempo runs your Google reviews for you. Every job asked, every review answered.

Supporting line for the VSL and checkout: connected to the job software you already use, live within a week, month to month.


## Step 3.1. Positioning statements

Against direct competitors (review software: NiceJob, Podium, the switch in your job software): Not "another review tool you have to run." Rempo connects to the software you already log jobs in and runs your review program for you: every job asked, every review answered with a reply written for it. You buy it online, at a published price, with no contract.

Against indirect competitors (marketing agencies and bundles such as Footbridge Media): Not "a marketing package with a review tool inside." Rempo runs one thing, your Google reviews, from your own job data, and shows you the numbers every month: requests sent, reviews in, replies posted. Add your profile or your website later if you want them.

Against the status quo (asking by hand, or not asking): Not "one more thing to remember after every job." Rempo asks every customer after every job and follows up, so asking no longer depends on anyone having a spare minute that day.

Every statement has been checked against GTM-BRIEF.md Section 5: no "only", no result claimed, no ranking promised, no staff blamed, and "done for you" is conditional on the CRM connection.


## What the VSL page has to carry

The funnel is not built in this run. These are the positioning requirements any page or video must meet:

Lead with the service. The first line says Rempo runs it for you, from the software they already use.

Show the request and a reply. A real personalized request (Rempo's own team, labeled as a sample) and a reply written for a real-looking review (labeled as a sample).

Say what the owner does once: connect the job software, give profile access, send a team photo. Keep the list short and true.

Price, setup fee, and guarantee on one screen, next to the buy button.

Answer the Level 2 objection ("we already have reviews in Jobber") in the FAQ.

Three calls to action, in the order of Phase 7: buy, book a call, take the scorecard.

No ranking promise, no review counts, no geotagging, no staff-naming request copy (GTM-BRIEF.md Section 5).
