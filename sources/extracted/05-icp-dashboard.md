| The checkout qualifier |
| The ICP becomes three questions at checkout. They protect the done-for-you promise and route buyers who do not fit to a bumper rather than losing them. |
| Question | Answer | Route |
| Where do you log your jobs? | Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or another CRM with customer phone or email | Continue to payment |
|  | Paper or spreadsheets | Offer the upload fallback ({DEFERRED: product}), or "Book a call" |
| How many jobs do you complete in a typical week? | 5 or more | Continue |
|  | Fewer than 5 | Continue, with a note that fewer jobs means fewer requests; offer the scorecard |
| How many locations? | 1 or 2 | Continue, priced per location |
|  | 3 or more | "Book a call" |
| The share of buyers who fail each question is a Phase 10 metric: it tells the founder whether the gate costs too many sales. |
