| Rempo go-to-market  ·  Assets and Collaterals Library |
| Channels and Hire.  Skill phase: Phase 8.  Source: dashboards/08-assets-library.md (Run 2, 2026-10-19). |
| The channel decision |
| # | Campaign | Role in the one funnel | Why this one |
| 1 | Meta ads (Facebook, Instagram) | Primary: creates demand and sends it to the VSL page or the scorecard | Most owners are not searching for review help; the request is a picture, and a feed is where a picture works; lead-form ads deliver the scorecard without a landing page |
| 2 | Google Search ads | Secondary: captures the owners already searching | Small budget; also teaches the words buyers use, for the VSL and FAQ |
| 3 | Nurture and retargeting | The middle of the funnel the founder asked for: scorecard opt-ins and VSL visitors who did not buy get email and retargeting until they buy, book a call, or leave | Turns traffic that is not ready into a list Rempo owns; the cheapest second chance at every paid visit |
| Later, not in the 90 days: field-service app marketplaces (Jobber and Housecall Pro list review apps) once the software connections exist; SEO comparison pages (written now for search ads, ranked later); partnerships with trade groups and web designers. |
| The hire: media buyer plus research strategist at $900 a month, not media buyer plus creative agency at $2,400 a month. Over 90 days the agency costs $7,200, 48% of the $15,000. The creative this funnel needs is modest and mostly founder-made: |
| •  One VSL, recorded by the founder, edited by a freelancer (one-time). |
| •  Ads: one lead message (Phase 3) with three hooks, in two formats (short video, static), refreshed about every four weeks: about 18 assets over 90 days. |
| •  The strongest creative is the product itself: a phone receiving a personalized request, and a reply written for a real-looking review. |
| The research strategist writes the hooks, the VSL outline, and the FAQ from Phase 3 and Phase 6. Revisit the agency at day 60 only if a hook is proven and creative fatigue becomes the limit. |
