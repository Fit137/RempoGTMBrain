| Step 9.1. Value metric selection |
| Denominator | Correlation to Value | Scalability | Ease of Measurement | Score (1-10) |
| /user | Low: value comes from customers asked, not staff logins | Poor: most shops have one or two users | Easy | 2.8 |
| /seat | Low: same as /user | Poor | Easy | 2.4 |
| /usage (customers asked per month) | High: each request is the work done | Good, but seasonal for HVAC and hard to predict at checkout | Medium: needs counting | 6.4 |
| /outcome (reviews received) | Highest on paper | Good | Medium | 1.9. Rejected: it ties Rempo's revenue to review volume, the incentive behind gating, and reads as a promised result |
| /capability (feature tiers) | Medium | Good | Easy | 5.2: tiers confuse a single-offer checkout |
| /location | High: each location is its own profile, photo, setup, and request stream | Excellent: grows with the customer | Easy | 8.9 |
| Recommendation |
| •  Primary value metric: per location. |
| •  Secondary usage metric: customers asked per location per month, as a fair-use limit ({TBD}), not a price driver. |
| •  Rationale: one number at checkout ($197 per location), arithmetic for shops with two locations, and a quote for three or more on a call. Capability moves out of tiers and into add-ons, which a checkout can sell one at a time. |

| Office staff ask and follow up by hand, 3 hours a week | $2,850 a year ($950 per weekly hour at the BLS median receptionist wage, $18.27/hour, May 2025) | 2663 | 1.1x in year 1; 1.2x from year 2 |

| Anchor 2. Alternative comparison |
| Alternative | Their Cost | Our Cost | Savings % |
| Podium Core (third-party reported list; annual terms) | About $399/month | $197/month | 0.506 |
| Done-for-you review services (agency guides) | $500 to $2,500/month | $197/month | 60.6% to 92.1% |
| Agency Google Business Profile management | $300 to $1,200/month | $197 + $97 = $294/month with GBP Upkeep | 2.0% to 75.5% |
| Footbridge Media (website and marketing bundle) | $249/month | $197/month | 20.9%, but Footbridge includes a website; the comparison is reviews run for you against a bundle with a review tool |
| NiceJob | $75 to $125/month | $197/month | None. The anchor is who does the work, not the price |
| The review switch in Jobber or Housecall Pro | Inside a $119 to $329/month plan | $197/month | None. Same point |
| Office staff, the status quo | $950 a year per weekly hour | $2,364 a year | Depends on hours |
| Against NiceJob and the job-software switch, Rempo costs more and should say so plainly: those are tools you run, and Rempo is a service that runs. |
