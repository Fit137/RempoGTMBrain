## Scorecard Specification (for ScoreApp)

Rempo go-to-market, Run 2. Skill phase: Lead Magnet.

2026-10-19

The skill's ScoreApp scorecard, and in Run 2 the middle-of-funnel asset the founder asked for: something a buyer who is not ready to purchase can consume, with an opt-in that puts them on the email list. It is built on the Phase 1 demand driver and leads to the free Google Review Gap Report.

All copy is checked against GTM-BRIEF.md Section 5: no promised reviews, rankings, or jobs; no staff blamed; every buy CTA carries the offer line from 06-value-proposition.md.


## Scorecard overview


## Questions

Higher score means a bigger gap. Questions 1 and 7 are unscored and used for routing. The five scored questions total 100.


## Results tiers

Routing overrides: paper or spreadsheets (question 1), or 3 or more locations (question 7), replace the CTA with "Book a 15-minute call", whatever the score. Every tier also offers the Gap Report.


## Post-scorecard email sequence

After email 4: one useful email a month (Phase 8, Campaign 3), each with the VSL link and the call link.


## Build notes

Data use: consent on the form for follow-up email, and a plain statement of what is done with the answers.

No incentives linked to reviews, for completing the scorecard or anywhere else.

Routing: the answers to questions 1, 3, and 7 pass into checkout, so a buyer who arrives from the scorecard is not asked twice (gtm-requirements-for-product.md).
