## Sheet: Gates

| Rempo go-to-market  ·  90-Day Experiment |
| Gates.  Skill phase: Phase 10 (added).  Source: dashboards/10-90-day-experiment.md (Run 2, 2026-10-19). |
| Definitions |
| •  Paid CAC: paid media spend ÷ new paying customers from paid channels, by cohort. |
| •  Self-serve share: purchases with no sales call before them ÷ all purchases. |
| •  Payback months: paid CAC ÷ monthly subscription revenue of the plans bought. |
| •  Minimum data before judging an ad set: about twice the CAC hurdle in spend ($700), a working rule of thumb. |
| Gates and decision rules |
| Thresholds come from payback arithmetic on the front-end price ($197 a month), so they hold before any bump or upsell revenue is proven. Recompute at Gate 2 with measured take rates. |
| Band | Payback | Front-end CAC |
| Green | 3 months or less | $591 or less |
| Yellow | 3 to 6 months | $591 to $1,182 |
| Red | More than 6 months | More than $1,182 |
| The founder's $350 working CAC sits inside green; reaching it is the stretch goal. |
| Gate | When | What is known by then | Decision rule |
| Gate 1 | Day 30 | First purchases; VSL completion; qualifier failure rates; first days-to-first-request | Red, or no purchase after $1,500 in a channel: change the hook or the VSL opening before spending more. Qualifier failure above {TBD} on the CRM question: name the supported software in every ad |
| Gate 2 | Day 60 | Two cohorts; hook test read; entry test under way; bump and upsell take rates | Red on a channel: stop it and move its budget. Green and at or below $350: scale with the reserve. Checkout starts high but purchases low, with price or "free trial" named in abandoned-checkout replies: run the Start test |
| Gate 3 | Day 90 | Three cohorts; month-2 retention of cohort 1; self-serve share; request-style results | Success and stop criteria below |
| Success and stop criteria |
| Success at day 90 (all four): |
| 1.  Blended paid CAC in green. |
| 2.  Self-serve share at or above the floor set in week 0. |
| 3.  Median days from software connection to first request of 7 or fewer (the guarantee holds). |
| 4.  Month-2 retention of the first cohort at or above the floor set in week 0. |
| Stop paid media if no channel reaches yellow by day 60. Put the remaining budget into the founding cohort's results and the scorecard list, and rework the VSL and offer before spending on ads again. |
| Stop selling without a call if the self-serve share is below half the floor at day 60 and call-to-purchase is green: the funnel keeps the VSL, but "Book a call" becomes the primary call to action until the page is reworked. |
