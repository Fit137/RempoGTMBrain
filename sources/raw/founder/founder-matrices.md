# The second briefing: Service Offer Matrix and Product Features Matrix

Supplied on 2026-10-19 as two CSV exports, kept verbatim in this folder:

- `service-offer-matrix-2026-10-19.csv`, the "Service & Offer Matrix" sheet (Sprint 1 briefing template)

This file transcribes the rows that carry a name. Wording and spelling are as supplied. The ratings are the founder's educated assumptions, not facts: the brief for this run says so explicitly, and `research/step-0d-service-outcomes.md` checks each one.

## Instructions that came with the briefing

From the request for this run, in summary: treat Rempo as a hybrid of service and product that is service-dominated, with the service as the front-end offer; one ICP, one funnel, one message, with product upsells inside the same funnel; a funnel optimized for self-serve purchase, with sales bumpers; a VSL at the front, FAQs, a purchase flow, and several calls to action, including a sales call for buyers who need more information and a middle-of-funnel asset with an opt-in to the email list. Do not build the funnel in this run. Do not take the dropdown options or the choices as the source of truth; research what each service actually produces.
