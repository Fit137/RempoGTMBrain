# Template, comparison page ("Rempo or [option]")

Audience: the owner evaluating options.
Funnel stage: evaluation. Linked from alternative-term search ads; also the first SEO assets (ranked later, not in the 90 days).
Load first: `../reference/competitor-battlecards.md`, `../reference/competitive-analysis.md`.
Pages planned: Rempo or NiceJob; Rempo or the Jobber review feature; Rempo or asking by hand.

## Sections
1. **Headline.** "Rempo or [option]: which fits your shop?" Neutral. No "vs" taunts.
2. **The short answer.** Two sentences: who should choose the other option, and who should choose Rempo. Lead with the other option's real strength.
3. **What each one is.** One line each. [Option] is software you run, or a bundle. Rempo is a service that runs your review program from your job software.
4. **Comparison table.** Rows only where both facts are known and sourced:
   | | [Option] | Rempo |
   |---|---|---|
   | Who runs it | [fact, source, date] | Rempo's team |
   | Price | [published or reported, source, date] | Per `../rules/feature-status.md` |
   | How you buy | [fact] | Online |
   | Requests after each job | [fact] | Once your job software is connected |
   | Replies | [fact] | A reply written for each review |
   | Contract | [fact, source] | Only the live terms |
5. **Who [option] suits.** Honest: shops that want to run it themselves, or already pay for it, or want a bundle.
6. **Who Rempo suits.** Owner-led home-service shops that log jobs in job software and want it run for them.
7. **One call to action.** "Watch how it works", plus the call link.
8. **Sources and date.** Every competitor fact with its source and the date it was checked.

## Fields
- Option: [NiceJob / Jobber review feature / asking by hand]
- Facts checked on: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Facts with sources: [list]

## Rules for comparisons
- No disparagement. State facts and who each option suits.
- Re-check competitor facts before publishing; they are dated 2026-10 in the brain.
- Podium's price is a third-party report and must be labeled as one.
- No "only" or "best" unless `../rules/do-not-say.md` lists the scoped form.

## Guardrails
Facts dated and sourced. No proof points. Check `../rules/do-not-say.md` (scoping section), `../rules/feature-status.md`, `../rules/glossary.md`, `../rules/writing-rules.md`, then `../ops/QA-checklist.md`.
