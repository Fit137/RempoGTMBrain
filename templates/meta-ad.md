# Template, Meta ad (Facebook and Instagram)

Audience: the owner of a home-service shop. Never the office manager in paid ads.
Funnel stage: awareness (cold), or retargeting (VSL viewers, scorecard takers).
Load first: `../reference/vsl-funnel-engine.md`, `../reference/marketing-campaigns.md`. Voice reference: `../examples/meta-ads.md`.

## Fields
- Placement: [Feed / Reels / Stories]
- Audience: [cold / VSL viewers / scorecard takers / offer reached, no purchase]
- Hook: [A "We run your Google reviews from your job software." / B "Here's what your customer sees." / C "Every review answered, every job asked."]
- Destination: [VSL page / scorecard]
- Primary text: [two to four short lines]
- Headline: [six words or fewer]
- Call to action button: [Learn more / Sign up]

## Video script, 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds
- 0 to 2 s HOOK: phone buzz and the request image, or the founder's one line. Spoken and on screen.
- 2 to 8 s PROBLEM: asking depends on whoever remembers.
- 8 to 22 s WHAT WE RUN: every job asked, a reminder, a reply written for each review. Once your job software is connected.
- CLOSE: one call to action. "Watch how it works."
- On-screen text (sound off): [three to six words carrying the point]
- Label: "Sample" on every request or reply shown.

## Static
- Image: the personalized request on a phone, labeled "Sample", or a reply to a sample review.
- Overlay: [hook line, six words or fewer]

## Scorecard lead ad (instant form or link)
- Primary text: "Most customers look for reviews from the last three months. How do yours look?" (BrightLocal 2026, source named in the ad or the form)
- Form: the scorecard questions or a link to them; email consent stated plainly.

## Retargeting variants
- VSL viewers: "What you do, what we do." Your four steps; ours.
- Offer reached, no purchase: price and terms only as `../rules/feature-status.md` allows; "Book a call if you have questions."
- Scorecard takers: "You scored [tier]. Here's how we fix the biggest gap for you." Tier name only, no personal data.

## Rules specific to Meta
- Ask or describe; never assert a fact about the viewer ("Your reviews are stale").
- No competitor names in ad text.
- The offer line goes in primary text only once DECIDED.

## Guardrails
No review, rating, or ranking promise; no claimed lift for personalized requests; no proof points; every statistic sourced. Check `../rules/do-not-say.md`, `../rules/feature-status.md`, `../rules/glossary.md`, `../rules/writing-rules.md` (writing for the ear), then `../ops/QA-checklist.md`.
