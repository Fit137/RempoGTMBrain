# Template, monthly report narrative (delivery copy)

Audience: the owner, with the office manager copied.
Stage: value realization and renewal (steps 5 to 7 of `../reference/customer-journey.md`).
Load first: `../reference/service-offer-matrix.md` (the KPI tree).

Done for you is invisible by design. The report makes the work visible. It reports, it never takes credit.

## Two blocks, always separate
**What we did (delivery, Rempo controls it):**
- Completed jobs received from your software: [n]
- Requests sent: [n]. Ask coverage: [requests ÷ completed jobs with a phone or email]%
- Reminders sent: [n]
- New reviews answered: [n of n], within 48 hours: [n]
- Days from software connection to first request (first report only): [n]

**What your profile shows (client outcome, reported, not promised):**
- Reviews in the last 90 days: [n], at signup: [baseline]
- Days since the latest review: [n], at signup: [baseline]
- Average rating, last 90 days: [x.x], lifetime: [x.x]
- Calls, website clicks, direction requests from your profile: [n], last month: [n] (where access allows)
- Local position on three service keywords: [average], at signup: [baseline]. Tracked, never promised.

## The narrative (four to six sentences)
1. What went out this month, in plain numbers.
2. What changed on the profile against the baseline, without saying Rempo caused it.
3. One thing worth noticing (a reply that mattered, a gap in jobs coming through).
4. One thing the shop can do, if any (a missing team photo, a connection check).
5. What happens next month.

## Never
- "We got you [n] reviews", "Rempo increased your rating", "you moved up because of us".
- A number without its comparison point.
- Product-layer pitches in the report. Upsells belong in the 30-day review call.
