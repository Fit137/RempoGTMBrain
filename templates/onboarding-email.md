# Template, onboarding emails

Audience: the office manager (copy the owner). The person who decides in month two whether Rempo stays.
Funnel stage: purchase through value realization (steps 5 to 7 of `../reference/customer-journey.md`).
Load first: `../reference/customer-journey.md` (activation blockers).

The one message: nothing new for you to run.

## The sequence
| # | When | Subject | Focus | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Right after payment | Welcome to Rempo. Here's your week. | What happens in the next 7 days; the four things we need; book the onboarding call | Book my onboarding call |
| 2 | 24 hours, if not booked | Pick a time for your setup call | One line, the calendar link | Book my onboarding call |
| 3 | Day 3, if a checklist item is missing | One thing we still need | The missing item, how to send it, how long it takes | [the item's link] |
| 4 | First request sent | Your first review requests just went out | What went out, to whom (counts only), what happens next | See a sample request |
| 5 | Day 30 | Your first month, and your 30-day review | The first monthly report; book the 30-day review | Book your 30-day review |

## The access checklist (in email 1)
1. Connect your job software. [system-specific steps, confirmed systems only]
2. Invite Rempo as a manager on your Google Business Profile. [steps]
3. Send one team photo. [what makes a good one]
4. Approve your texting registration details. [what we need, why it takes about a week]

## The 7-day clock
Explain it only if the guarantee is DECIDED: the clock starts at the software connection, because the connection depends on you. Email requests can start before texts clear.

## Each email
- Subject: what happens, not a greeting.
- Body: one task or one update. Short.
- Signed by the founder or the assistant, with a reply-to that reaches a person.
