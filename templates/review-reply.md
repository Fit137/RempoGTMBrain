# Template, review reply (delivery copy)

Audience: the reviewer, and every future customer reading the profile. Posted publicly under the client's name.
Stage: delivery, every new review. Approval mode or automatic posting: `{DEFERRED: product}`, set per account.
Load first: `../reference/review-compliance.md`. Voice reference: `../examples/review-request-and-replies.md`.

Every reply is written for the review in front of it. No fixed template text: 50% of consumers are put off by generic or templated replies (BrightLocal 2026).

## Fields
- Reviewer first name: [as shown on Google, first name only]
- What they mentioned: [the specific job, problem, or person they named]
- Star rating: [1 to 5]
- Shop voice: [the client's tone notes]
- Signed: [owner name or "The [shop name] team"]

## Five stars (2 to 3 sentences)
Thank them by first name. Mention one specific thing they said. Close warmly, no sales pitch.

## Three or four stars (3 to 4 sentences)
Thank them. Acknowledge the specific thing that fell short, in their terms. Say what the shop does about it, only if the client confirms it. Offer a direct line if they want to talk.

## One or two stars (3 to 4 sentences)
Calm, no argument, no excuses. Acknowledge their experience. Invite them to call or email a named person directly. Do not discuss details in public.

## Never in a reply
- Details the reviewer did not share: address, invoice, job specifics, health or personal information.
- Argument, blame, or "that's not what happened".
- An offer in exchange for changing or removing the review.
- A staff member's last name, or a staff member blamed.
- Keywords stuffed for search.
- The same sentence used in another reply this month.

## Alerts
A one or two star review triggers an alert to the owner. Whether the alert carries the drafted reply is PARTIAL in `../rules/feature-status.md`; until confirmed, send the draft separately. The owner decides whether to call the customer first.
