# Template, review request and reminder (delivery copy)

Audience: the client's customers. Sent under the client's name, from Rempo's setup.
Stage: delivery, after each completed job.
Load first: `../reference/review-compliance.md`, the delivery section of `../rules/do-not-say.md`. Voice reference: `../examples/review-request-and-replies.md`.

Every customer gets the same request. No satisfaction check first, no filtering, no reward, no staff name request, no suggested wording or star prompt.

## Fields
- Shop name: [as on the Google profile]
- Sender: [owner or team name, as the client approves]
- Team photo: [the client's own photo; the image merges the customer's first name]
- First-name fallback: [no name on file: "Hi there"]
- Google review link: [the client's own link]
- Timing: [same day / day 3, per the account's test]
- Opt-out line (text): "Reply STOP to opt out."

## Text, first request (under 300 characters)
"Hi [first name], thanks for choosing [shop name] today. Would you share an honest review of your visit? It helps neighbors choose. [link] Reply STOP to opt out."
Image: the team photo with the customer's first name.

## Text, one reminder (only if no review, after [n] days)
"Hi [first name], a quick reminder from [shop name]. If you have a minute, we'd appreciate an honest review. [link] Reply STOP to opt out."

## Email, first request
- Subject: "Thanks from [shop name]"
- Body: the image, then two sentences: thanks for the job; would you share an honest review. The link as a button: "Leave a review".
- Footer: the shop's address and an unsubscribe link.

## Never
- "Mention [technician]!" or any staff name.
- "Leave us 5 stars", any star or wording suggestion.
- Discounts, entries, or gifts.
- A "How did we do?" gate that routes only happy customers to Google.
- More than one reminder per job.
- A second request to a customer who already reviewed.
