# Template, sales call guide (15 minutes)

Audience: the owner who booked a call from the VSL page, the scorecard, or a checkout route (no job software, or 3 or more locations).
Funnel stage: evaluation and purchase. Run by the founder; later the assistant.
Load first: `../reference/objection-handling.md`, `../reference/icp-personas.md`, `faq.md`.

Spoken, so write for the ear: short lines, second person.

## 0 to 2 minutes, set the call
"Thanks for booking. Fifteen minutes: I'll ask three questions, answer yours, and if it fits, send you the checkout link. If it doesn't fit, I'll say so."

## 2 to 6 minutes, fit check (the three qualifier questions)
1. Where do you log your jobs? [system name]. Confirmed systems only per `../rules/feature-status.md`. No software: explain the fallback status honestly (`{DEFERRED: product}`).
2. About how many jobs a week? Fewer than 5: say plainly that fewer jobs means fewer requests.
3. How many locations? 3 or more: priced per location, quoted on this call (once the offer is DECIDED).
Note the review position: [newest review date, reply habit, how they compare locally].

## 6 to 11 minutes, their questions
Answer from `faq.md` and `../reference/objection-handling.md`. Write down every question in their words for `../ops/signal-log.md`.

## 11 to 14 minutes, the offer
- What we run, what you do once.
- Price and terms only as `../rules/feature-status.md` allows today.
- What we don't promise: review counts, ratings, rankings.

## 14 to 15 minutes, next step
- Fits: "I'll send the checkout link now, same offer as the page."
- Not yet: "I'll send the scorecard and the reply guide. No follow-up calls unless you ask."
- Doesn't fit: say why, and what would make it fit.

## After the call
- Log objections and buyer language in `../ops/signal-log.md`, verbatim.
- Send the checkout link or the scorecard within the hour.

## Guardrails
No pressure language, no discount invented on the call, no promise beyond the page. Check `../rules/do-not-say.md`, `../rules/feature-status.md`, `../rules/glossary.md`, `../rules/writing-rules.md` (writing for the ear), then `../ops/QA-checklist.md`.
