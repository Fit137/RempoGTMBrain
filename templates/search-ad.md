# Template, Google Search ads

Audience: the owner who is already searching.
Funnel stage: awareness (problem terms), discovery and evaluation (solution, brand, and alternative terms).
Load first: `../reference/marketing-campaigns.md`. Voice reference: `../examples/search-ads.md`.

## Term groups and destinations
| Group | Example terms | Destination | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem | how to get more google reviews; google review request text | Scorecard | The free scorecard, not the product |
| Solution | review management for contractors; plumber review software; automated google review requests | VSL page | Done for you, from your job software |
| Brand | rempo | VSL page | The core claim |
| Alternative | podium alternative; nicejob alternative | VSL page or comparison page | Run for you, published price |

Competitor names appear as keywords only. Never in headlines or descriptions. Check the trademark policy before launch.

## Responsive search ad fields
- Headlines (up to 15, 30 characters each): [pull from the copy bank; mix done-for-you, personal, and the CTA]
- Descriptions (up to 4, 90 characters each): [what Rempo runs; what you do once; one CTA]
- Pins: [pin the core claim or the scorecard offer to position 1 only if testing shows a need]
- Final URL and path: [rempo.io/ + short path]
- Sitelinks: [How it works / FAQ / Take the scorecard / Book a call]

## Writing for search
- Mirror the searcher's words in the first headline, then answer with the process.
- A headline must make sense alone; any can show without the others.
- No headline promises reviews, a count, a rating, or a ranking, even though the searcher's term asks for "more reviews".
- Problem-term ads offer the scorecard. They do not sell the product.
- Prices and the guarantee appear only once DECIDED.

## Negative keywords to start
[free google reviews] [buy google reviews] [remove google reviews] [fake reviews] [jobs] [salary]
