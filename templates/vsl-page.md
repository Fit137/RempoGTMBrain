# Template, VSL page

Audience: the owner. One audience per page.
Funnel stage: discovery, evaluation, and the step into checkout (step 2 of `../reference/customer-journey.md`).
Load first: `../reference/vsl-funnel-engine.md`, `../reference/value-proposition-owner.md`. Voice reference: `../examples/vsl-page-hero.md`.

## Sections, top to bottom
1. **Hero.** Headline from the core claim in `../ops/copy-bank.md`. Subhead: what it is and who it is for (home-service shops that log jobs in job software). The video directly under it. Primary button: "Get started". Secondary text link: "Book a 15-minute call".
2. **The problem, in their words.** Two or three sentences: asking depends on memory; the newest review is old; replies go unwritten. No product yet.
3. **What you do once / what we do.** Two columns. Owner: connect your job software, give Google profile access, send a team photo, approve texting details. Rempo: everything else, listed as plain steps. Office manager line: "Nothing new for you to run."
4. **Here's what your customer sees.** The personalized request image, labeled "Sample". Then a reply to a sample review, labeled "Sample".
5. **The three pillars.** VP 1 done for you, VP 2 personal not templated, VP 3 proof you can see. Headline plus one body line each, from the copy bank. Status flag in line on anything PARTIAL or PLANNED.
6. **What we don't do.** Remove reviews, filter unhappy customers, reward reviews, ask customers to name staff, promise rankings or review counts. One line on why: Google's rules and the FTC's.
7. **Price block.** Price, setup fee, and guarantee on one screen, beside the buy button.
   - If the offer is DECIDED: the offer line, verbatim.
   - If not: `[OFFER PENDING]`. Do not fill it with Run 2 prices. Do not reuse "Start Free" without the setup fee beside it.
8. **FAQ.** From `faq.md`. Include the Level 2 question: "We already have reviews in Jobber."
9. **Three calls to action, in this order.** Get started. Book a 15-minute call. Not ready? Take the scorecard.
10. **Footer note.** Sources for every statistic on the page, dated.

## Exit and scroll
Exit intent and deep scroll without a click offer the scorecard, never a discount.
