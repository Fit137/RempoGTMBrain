# Template, VSL script (spoken, 5 to 8 minutes)

Audience: the owner. Never blended with office manager copy.
Funnel stage: discovery and evaluation (step 2 of `../reference/customer-journey.md`).
Load first: `../reference/vsl-funnel-engine.md`. Voice reference: `../examples/vsl-opening.md`.
Recorded by the founder; edited by a freelancer; outline by the research strategist.

## Phase 1, plan (fill before writing a word)
- Hook in use: [A done for you / B here's what your customer sees / C every review answered]
- One-sentence takeaway: [what the owner should believe at the end]
- Sample assets ready: [the personalized request built with Rempo's own team; a reply to a sample review]
- Offer status: [DECIDED or `[OFFER PENDING]` from `../ops/decisions.md`]
- Statistics used, each with its source: [max three]

## Phase 2, write (for the ear)
1. **Hook (0:00 to 0:20).** The tension in one line: the newest review is old, or asking depends on whoever remembers. No "welcome", no name first.
2. **Why it matters (to 1:00).** What buyers read on a profile: recency and replies. One or two cited facts, said and shown on screen with the source.
3. **What Rempo runs (to 2:00).** Every job asked, a reminder, a reply written for each review, a monthly report. The core claim, said once, plainly.
4. **Show it (to 3:00).** The personalized request arriving on a phone, labeled "sample". A reply to a sample review, labeled "sample". No claimed lift.
5. **What you do once (to 3:45).** Connect your job software, give Google profile access, send a team photo, approve the texting details. Then: "That's it. We do the rest."
6. **What it is not (to 4:15).** We don't remove reviews, filter customers, offer rewards for reviews, or promise rankings. Say why: Google's rules.
7. **The offer (to 5:30).** Price, setup fee, and guarantee only as `../rules/feature-status.md` allows. If the offer is not DECIDED, read `[OFFER PENDING]` in the draft and stop there.
8. **Three choices (to the end).** Get started. Or book a 15-minute call. Or not ready: take the scorecard. Same order every time.

Mark pauses and on-screen notes inline: [pause], [on screen: sample request], [on screen: source].

## Phase 3, edit
- Cut every warm-up line before the hook.
- One idea per beat. If a beat needs two, split it or cut one.
- Read it aloud. Any sentence that runs out of breath gets cut in two.
- Check every capability against the status ledger, every superlative against the claim ledger.
- Confirm the "once your software is connected" condition survived the edit.

## Phase 4, metadata
Chapters at each beat; captions on; the sources list in the description.

## Guardrails
No result promised, no ranking, no proof points, no PLANNED item shown working. Samples labeled. Run `../rules/writing-rules.md` (writing for the ear), `../rules/do-not-say.md`, `../rules/feature-status.md`, `../rules/glossary.md`, then `../ops/QA-checklist.md`.
